### 2.3 How decisions without an owner trace enter the repo

Owner answers are given in session chat and reach the repo only through an agent. The agent writes a paraphrase into a batch commit ("the owner's eight decisions") and a bare "(owner, date)" tag into the doc, without the question or the words, so a later reader cannot tell the owner's choice from the agent's recommendation that preceded it (M6), and the tag cannot be checked. Next to these, agents record their own judgements in decision vocabulary ("accepted", "settled", "by design", "don't re-propose") with no actor at all (M1); a reader, and later agents, take these as decided and obey them. The text then drifts during migration from a temporary plan into the permanent docs and during compaction or comment-cap passes: qualifiers such as "for now", "on current code", "deferred to the refactor", "until hardware exists" and "not one of the original twenty" are dropped, a one-item answer is generalised into a rule, a plan-scoped exclusion becomes permanent, and owner tags are dropped from real owner decisions or placed over agent additions (M3, M4). Finally the temporary plans that held the owner's words (FINAL_WIRING_PLAN.md, WP_RESTART_HANDOVER.md, WEBSITE_PLAN.md, BUILD_CHAIN_PLAN.md, UART_PROMOTION_REQUIREMENTS.md) are deleted, leaving numbered labels and "owner" tags whose source can only be recovered from git history (list E).

### 2.4 Prevention rules for future sessions

1. Every decision statement names its actor and date, "(owner, YYYY-MM-DD)" or "(agent, YYYY-MM-DD)"; decision vocabulary without an actor is not written.
2. An owner decision is recorded with the owner's words quoted, the question included, in the commit message and in the doc; a paraphrase is marked as one, and an agent's recommendation stays labelled as the agent's even when the owner accepts it.
3. Compaction, migration, proofreading and comment-cap commits may not change actors, qualifiers ("for now", "until …", "on current code"), scope or foreclosure words; a moved rule keeps its tag and scope, and the commit says "no meaning change" or names the change.
4. A question for the owner is asked in the session or entered in one owner-question list in BACKLOG.md with its date; "flagged for the owner", "owner's call" and "revisit" do not appear anywhere else, and no doc or comment cites a temporary plan by section or number.
5. A `tests_scripts/` check fails on actorless decision vocabulary (accepted, settled, decided, by design, deliberate, don't re-propose, never) in docs and comments unless the sentence carries an actor tag or a source citation, and on any citation to a missing file, heading or numbered decision.
