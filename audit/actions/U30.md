**Object sizes used below** (rp2, v1.29.0; 16-byte GC block, `py/mpconfig.h:250-251` with 4-byte words): a tuple of n
items is 8 + 4n bytes (`py/objtuple.h:34-38`); a heap int is one 16-byte `mp_obj_int_t` block (`py/objint.h:32-38`,
`py/mpz.h:93-100`) plus a digit array of at least `MIN_ALLOC` = 2 16-bit digits (`py/mpz.c:605, 678-690`), one block;
a float is one block (`py/objfloat.c:182-190`, boxed under `MICROPY_OBJ_REPR_A`, `py/mpconfig.h:163`); every binary
operation with a heap-int operand allocates its result object and digits, even when the result then fits a small int
(`py/objint_mpz.c:215, 309-317`).
