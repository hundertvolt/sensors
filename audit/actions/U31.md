## Open points
**Q1. May the supervisor feed once more before a budget reboot?** (settles A.U31.07)
- (a) Yes: at the first task end past the budget it feeds once, then stops feeding one-way and arms the reboot; the
  reboot always lands before the watchdog, FRAM paused. **Recommended.**
- (b) No: the scan stops at that end but does not feed; under load the watchdog may reset the unit first, mid-write,
  and a module's FRAM history may be lost (OR69.a (1) all-or-nothing).
