**Names this unit introduces (B1 foundation):**

- **Tag** `@tunable <id> = <literal>` in a `#` or `//` comment on its own line, directly above the
  line that carries `<literal>` as a whole token; several tags may stack above one line. `<id>` is
  `<area>.<name>`, lower-case, matching `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*\.[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`.
- **Register**: new `SPECIFICATION.md` **Part N "Tunable parameters"**, its tables between the
  markers `<!-- tunables:begin -->` and `<!-- tunables:end -->`; row columns
  `ID | Value | Sites (file — literal) | Dependants | Basis | Margin | Re-check trigger`; row kinds
  `tuned` (tagged) and `rule` (a bound with no single code site, untagged, names what checks it).
- **Check**: `tests_scripts/test_tunables_register.py` (L0).
- **Classes a candidate falls into** (N.1, from OR30.a (1)): *tuned* (tagged); *derived* (computed
  from a tuned value or a fact, not tagged, its source named in the source row's Dependants);
  *contract* (UART wire constants and recovery timings, `payload_size`/`timeout`/baud by
  out-of-band agreement, FRAM layout incl. `history_length`; excluded, OR30.a (1)); *fact*

  addresses, counts of fields); *config*, *test input* and *mirror* as A.U8.01 defines them (V.U8.02).
