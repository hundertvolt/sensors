# U20 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-08

Unit U20: the build generator and the device definitions. Every device's boot entry is generated: it arms the watchdog
as its first statement, puts `.frozen` first on `sys.path`, reserves the emergency exception buffer and calls the
generated `main()`, which runs the setup list, starts the tasks, the timers and NTP, then the supervisor; a no-autostart
variant is generated beside it. `SystemService` gains one command gate and a controlled shutdown sequence for reboot,
bootloader and the two new words `resetconfig` and `erasefram`; the config stores close at a command's acceptance, a
deferred write is flushed under each store's `owner_lock`, and reset record 9 marks a sequence that never reached its
arm. Every build error names its rule, place and fix and is proven through the CLI; the TOMLs are checked by
`build_model()` alone, with pin legality per GPIO, range checks on every numeric field and a fuzz; the tag parsers
refuse `nan`/`inf` and register families by lookup; the frozen set is seeded from the generated module's imports; the
build reads and writes UTF-8 under any locale. Twelve lanes on disjoint files; the lead merged them, fixed what the
merge made red, shrank the allow-lists, applied lane K's last patch after review and root-caused the web tier's failure
to the twin's SCD30 fake.
