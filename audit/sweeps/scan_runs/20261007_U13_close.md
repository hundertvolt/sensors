# U13 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-07

Unit U13: the bus layers. An I2C register write is one `writeto()` with its count tested and a register read a no-stop
address write then `readfrom_into()`, so a NACKed byte is `EIO`; the I2C wrapper clears a wedged bus at construction and
on demand, waiting for SCL in both clears, re-constructs the controller in `recover()` and answers bool or `None`; the
build bounds the I2C timeout and the boot clears' unfed time. SPI and FRAM answer bool, a down bus is `FRAM_BUS_DOWN`.
The reader base gains the recovery ladder, inert in every driver until U15. Each UART link receives through a DMA ring;
a lap or a UARTRSR OE, FE or BE fails the frame; writes go only into an empty TX ring. Both `machine` fakes and the new
`rp2` fakes model the bus and UART registers, with bounded logs of fixed occupancy. Four lanes; the lead merged them,
fixed what only the merged tree showed, re-merged onto the final U11 and U12 and ran the whole gate.
