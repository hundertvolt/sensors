# U17 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-07

Unit U17: the UART protocol module, its bus driver and codec, the link exerciser, their tests, the hazard tiers, the twin
link, the device scripts, the build checks and the C-port changelog. Received data is assembled in bounded pieces
(`PieceBuffer`) under a per-instance cap, with the limits grouped in one `TransferLimits`; the receive ring has a floor
sized for the flash-write hold; failed reads count the bytes they drop, which feeds the mismatched-peer diagnostic;
overruns, ring refusals and stalled writes are reported; a GET with CHUNKS other than 1 and a train over the cap are
refused, the responder re-listens at once after a validated command, and a stale hold-off expires. Every timing
judgement in the UART and I2C test tiers runs on a poll-round clock. The wire format is untouched (two receiver-only
tightenings and one recovery change emitting no byte, Class A A14-A16). Ten lanes on disjoint files; the lead merged
them, converted the last old-keyword caller and shrank the pending lists and the explicit-Any baseline.
