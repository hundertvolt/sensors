# U15 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-07

Unit U15: the sensor drivers. All four drivers move onto the shared base: one typed `DeviceSession`, the shared read-trigger
divider and timer-fault path, and the recovery ladder wired (each sets its recovery bus, runs the controller rung on a
chip-setup failure and supplies its participant rung). BMP3XX reads EVENT and ERR_REG every cycle, re-applies its stored
configuration after a chip self-reset and waits the computed conversion time. SCD30 becomes a config reader with a
RAM-only config log and an FRC readiness state, gates readings to the datasheet ranges and warns on a stuck data-ready
line. ISL29125 compares its shadow every cycle, parks and re-arms its INT, writes thresholds under one lock, publishes
`CalLight` and keeps its stored ticks inside the horizon. SGP40 reads its serial as three CRC-checked words, turns its
heater off as its rung, keeps the backup on a failed restore cycle and skips stale compensation. New codes E27, W11,
W71, W73, W75 and a `status` table section; the hazard tiers follow the four end states; a new driver-shape check. Four
lanes on disjoint files; the lead merged them and fixed the cross-lane reds.
