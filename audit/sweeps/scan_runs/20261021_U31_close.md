# U31 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-21

Unit U31: PERF, the timing budget applied to every layer. Every `src/` wait takes whole milliseconds: `asyncio.sleep_ms()`
and `wait_for_ms()` throughout, held by `tests_scripts/test_src_sleep_forms.py` (four named synchronous waits) and, in the
unit tier, by every one-yield stand-in running the real `sleep_ms()`'s own integer check (FX2); 20 Part N tags renamed from
`_s` to `_ms`, every value kept but BMP3XX's reset settle (2 to 3 ms) and its conversion wait (rounded up). The build
refuses an I2C timeout whose double exceeds the device's smallest hard consumer (`bus.i2c-timeout-consumer`; one export,
`buildgen.validate.smallest_hard_consumer()`, serves the rule, the twin scenario and the bench test: dev 925 ms, the others
6000 ms). The supervisor records its own fault once (E115 `SUPERVISOR_RAISED`, OF-26); its escalation pre-writes the reset
record and bounds its entry write at 1200 ms, a timeout and a full heap included, so a held lock or an exhausted heap still
reboots with code 20 or 5 (OF-222, RA-1); the uptime and WiFi re-arms print once per episode (OF-29). The twin's STA
refuses `connect()` while inactive (EPERM) and loses its link when the access point goes down or the STA is deactivated
(OF-217, OF-218); two dropped one-shot fires are scenarios (OF-198), and the loop's lag under combined load is held to the
smallest hard consumer on every device (the runner's `--test-loop-lag-ms` and `--test-drop-timer-fire`). SPECIFICATION.md
F.3 holds the timing budget as one table (9 consumer and 27 budget rows at `b27f485`), checked by
`tests_scripts/test_timing_budget.py`; a setup unit takes no timed wait but a UART link's boot drain (A.7, the boot
scenario). An independent review of the unit's diff found 53 defects, 8 of them blocking; each went to a fix block (FX1 to
FX6) or the lead, or was accepted on a ruling.
`src/` changed in 11 files at `b27f485` (`git diff aba11d5 b27f485 -- src`: 181 insertions, 157 deletions); no UART
protocol file and no changelog entry changed, so no wire byte did, and nothing ran on hardware. Since the base `aba11d5`:
at `b27f485`, before FX4, FX5, FX6 and the lead's last Part N edits, 66 files outside `arduino/` and `datasheets/`, 2375
insertions, 534 deletions, 3 of them new; at the final head 76 files (the evidence aside), 3085 insertions, 732
