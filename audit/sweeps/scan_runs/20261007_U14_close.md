# U14 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-07

Unit U14: the platform facts. A 2**30-period fake `time` and an L0 source scan of every tick user prove the tick
arithmetic across rp2's wrap; every tick function is crossed over a real wrap against the same call far from it. The
NTP client turns an allocation failure while parsing a reply into the shared ALLOC code and a missed response. The
FRAM log store packs and writes its live state under one lock, skipping a call only once an earlier successful write
carried its state. Part F states the platform facts against the pin and the datasheets; the new F.7 lists the Unix-port
rig's divergences from rp2, rows 15-22 from a diff of the effective `MICROPY_*` settings of the three builds. Four lanes
on disjoint files; the lead ran the settings diff, merged the lanes onto U11-U13 and restored one owner decision's scope.
