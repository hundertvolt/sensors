# U18 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-08

Unit U18: the Wi-Fi service, captive DNS, the DNS client, the NTP client, the UDP socket, their tests, the twin UDP
shim, the build and website tags carrying network fields, and the console line of a layer with no logger. The Wi-Fi
service publishes one 8-field snapshot and loses its three radio getters; its state machine persists every transition,
keeps an empty SSID out of the failure streak, leaves the retry wait unlocked, and runs one LED flash task with a
pattern for a phone on the hotspot. Captive DNS answers A/ANY only and drops malformed queries; the resolver tries only
its caller's servers and detects a cut reply; NTP gains a configurable `DNSFallback`, a staleness that is reachable and
a 48-byte exchange of its own. `UDPSocket` takes tuples only, connects once per call and polls at two rates.
`console(name, what, err=None)` prints a caught `MemoryError`'s text in every layer with no logger, heap-free. Eleven
lanes on disjoint files; the lead merged them, fixed the comments U18 made stale outside a lane's files, and shrank the
allow-lists.
