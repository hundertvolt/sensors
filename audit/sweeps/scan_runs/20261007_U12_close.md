# U12 close scan (silent_failure_scan.md), 2026-10-07

Unit U12: the algorithms. The CRC engine runs CRC32 as two small-int words and pass mode checks its bounds; the frame
codecs drop a test-only counter; a zero-length UART payload is nothing to transfer in every CRC and codec mode. The VOC
port's fix16 helpers wrap to int32 as the C reference does, pinned by Sensirion's own vectors (helpers and the whole index
sequence); its persisted state is little-endian and range-checked. The SGP40 measure command is staged inside the device
session. The math helpers gain the wet-bulb corner gate, `pressure_at_height()` and refusing humidity, whose water pair
is the published one. Four lanes on disjoint files; the lead merged them onto U11, fixed what only the merged tree showed
and ran the whole gate.
