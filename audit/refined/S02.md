# Refined harvest S02 — RP02 (HEAD 5efc919)

Sweep S2 "Rule drift over history" (PATTERNS.md section 1). Worked at checkout `c3e3686`, whose only
change over `5efc919` is `audit/refined/PATTERNS.md`; every anchor below is valid at `5efc919`.
Method: a per-commit history miner over all 1,601 non-merge commits (merges are S1/RP03), prose of every
`*.md` outside `audit/`/the plan/legacy/`arduino/`/`ext/`, and the comments and docstrings of `src/`,
`buildgen/`, `tests*/`, `digital_twin/`, `scripts/`, `toolchain/`, `js/`, `devices/`, `.github/`, the
config files. For each commit, each removed sentence is paired with its closest added sentence in the
same commit (Dice ≥ 0.45, any file, so moves and plan migrations are caught). The miner flags lost
qualifiers, lost reasons and lost or added actor tags, added foreclosure words and added universals. A
flag is kept only when the drifted wording still stands at HEAD. Deleted plans were also compared
directly with HEAD text (29 hits). Then every kept flag was triaged by hand, and each line below was
checked at its site and its introducing or dropping commit (`git show`, `git log -S`). Dedup followed
PATTERNS 0.2.

Owner-matter follows OR13.a for drift in an owner rule's scope, and harmonization 27 / OR68.a (2) for a
lost owner tag with a checkable trail (restored, not re-asked, so `n`). "Owner tag" means an owner
attribution the repo wrote down. It is not a claim that the owner said it, unless owner words are quoted.
