U22 lane W WIP W1: the silent mode, the fresh-boot reconnect, the stations rule

The second failed STA streak after the hotspot ran no longer switches Wi-Fi off: it enters a
silent phase (_PHASE_SILENT = 4) with no hotspot and the LED dark, persists one WLAN_SILENT
(W80, its number kept), and makes one connect attempt per hotspot_time_min on the window's own
PERIODIC timer, uncapped, until a link; WLAN.scan() is never used. A credentials change
(reconnect_wifi(), the identity group's post hook) lands in the fresh-boot state; the window end
keeps its meaning. A stations check that cannot confirm a client (a raise, or the chip-down
32-entry answer) counts as no client and persists WLAN_STATIONS_UNKNOWN.

Decided at execution (agent, 2026-10-08), each conservative and reversible:
- the silent attempt is a method of its own, _run_silent_mode(), dispatched by _connect_loop()
  beside _run_hotspot_mode()/_run_sta_mode(), so _run_sta_mode() keeps its seeking meaning;
- the dark guard reads the phase inside _led_on(), _led_toggle() and _run_led_pattern() rather
  than holding the LED handle back, since _attempt_sta_connect() and the LEDWifiOn push re-select
  the handle on every attempt;
- the window's arm is extracted into _arm_hotspot_timer() -> bool (async: it persists the TIMER
  fault), shared by _hotspot_client_absent() and _run_silent_mode(), one arm path, one wording;
- the private window-end trigger is _on_hotspot_window_end(); reconnect_wifi() is that teardown
  plus the fresh-boot flag _reconnect_fresh; the watcher sets _silent_attempt_due while silent;
- a task restart keeps the silent phase, and so does the restart's own plain reconnect;
  _on_sta_disconnected() registers no failure while silent.

Catalog: W80 renamed WLAN_SILENT with its new text, E65 WLAN_OFF retired (its one site goes),
W77's text names the counted-as-no-client rule, and the status table NotifyState is added.
The fault-or-warning table loses the permanent deactivation's pair. Tests written first and seen
failing on the tree; the HEAD tests the change turns red are rewritten in place.
