U22 lane W: a silent period that cannot be armed feeds the hardware streak

In the silent mode a failed period arm (Timer.init() refusing with ENOMEM) was persisted and
retried every iteration but never set _hw_op_failed. With no period no attempt is ever due, so
the streak gate skipped _error_check() and the unit stayed silent with no escalation until a power
cycle. The failed arm is now the iteration's hardware failure, as a failed uptime-tick re-arm in
the same loop already is, so the streak reaches its device rung and its give-up, and the
supervisor's restart keeps the silent mode (agent, 2026-10-12). _arm_hotspot_timer() and the
hotspot mode's caller are unchanged.

b283d81 (400 loop iterations, history WLAN_SILENT then TIMER only, no rung, no give-up).
