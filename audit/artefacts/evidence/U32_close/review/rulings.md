## The owner question (BACKLOG.md owner question 7, entered 2026-10-21, agent)
7. **Keep timestamping once the NTP sync goes stale?** The sync turns stale when none has succeeded for 3 x `NTPInterval` (36 h at the default 12 h), and an NTP settings change clears it until the next sync (owner, 2026-09-29); the RTC keeps good time meanwhile. Two consumers then drop the clock: `/status` `UTCTime` reads null, and the VOC backup (the SGP40's algorithm state in FRAM, on all six devices: SGP40 on I2C, FRAM on SPI) is written without a timestamp, logging warning 35 at each backup, and such a backup is restored at the next boot with no age test. `LocalTime` and the LED schedule already keep the clock set this boot (agent, 2026-10-06). (a) keep it so, as legacy did: nothing changes. (b) both keep the clock set this boot through a stale sync: `UTCTime` stays shown, backups keep their timestamp and their age test; after an NTP settings change both still wait for the next sync. A firmware change in the generated module and the NTP client, the same on every device; no bus traffic or wiring changes. Until answered: (a), the current behaviour.
