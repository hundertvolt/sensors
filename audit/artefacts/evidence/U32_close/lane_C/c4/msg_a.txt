U32 lane C: the reset and shutdown tasks end in a top of their own

SystemService's two unsupervised tasks, _reset_when_due() and _shutdown_sequence(), now end in the
top the other unsupervised tasks have: `except Exception` with report_if_fatal() and SYSTEM's
err_s() under the shared UNEXPECTED code. The outcome is unchanged: no feed follows, so the
watchdog resets; the record reads the escalation's or the command's code, 9 for a sequence that
stopped before its arm. FRAM is paused before the reset task runs and after the shutdown's
quiesce, so those failures reach the console and the RAM log only.

Owner decision (owner, 2026-10-21: "The task logs shall then be handled by their creators in
general and the caveats are okay as they are inevitable."; the question, paraphrase: whether a
task's failure is logged by the module creating it, five tasks already doing so and two in
SystemService needing a small change, with FRAM paused before a reset and the logging call itself
as the two caveats).

Tests first, each seen failing on the old module: a raise injected into the reset task's last
flush and into a config reset's delete step escaped the task; now its cause is logged, no reset
action runs or is armed, and nothing feeds.
