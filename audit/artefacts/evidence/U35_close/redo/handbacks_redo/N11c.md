## Item 2: the scanner's six blind shapes

`tools/waits2.py` (scratch, never committed) runs v1 (N10's `untagged_waits.py`, unchanged) as its first stage, then
traces values across `tests/`, `digital_twin/` and `src/`. A call resolves through the file's own defs, its
`from X import` names, its `import X` modules and the methods of classes it defines or imports. A value is traced
through parameters, local and module assignments, and `self.x` attributes, to a fixpoint (6 passes on the tree).
Sites are reported for `tests/*.py` only. Shapes:
1. s1, a positional argument into a call whose parameter reaches a wait, and a default of such a parameter.
2. s2, an assignment `obj.X = n` (or a class attribute) where X is waited on: a module constant read at a wait, or a
   `self.X` read at one. A `ticks_diff()` compare inside an `if`/`while` counts as a wait (`dl` when the literal sits
   there itself).
3. s3, `period=`/`freq=` on `Timer(...)`/`.init(...)`.
4. s4, the `range()` count of a `for` loop whose body sleeps or waits (tests/ helpers only; an attribute count
   propagates only as `self.x`).
5. s5, a keyword into a parameter that reaches a wait under another name; on a call nothing resolves, any keyword
   matching `timeout|deadline|_ms$|_s$|_us$|_tries$|_polls$|period` (`s5r`).
6. s6, a constant or local reaching a wait through arithmetic, a call or another assignment.
