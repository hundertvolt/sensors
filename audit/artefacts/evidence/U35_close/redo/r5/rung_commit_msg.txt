U35: the controller rung runs once per bus per episode, whichever reader reaches it

A reader skipped the controller rung whenever the bus's one recoveries count had moved since its
episode began, so a clear run by another reader that then stopped failing used up every other
reader's controller rung and no rebuild ran. The bus now also counts its rebuilds, and a reader
skips the controller rung only if a rebuild ran since its episode began; the clear rung's check
stays on recoveries, which a rebuild steps too. Mock tests pin both halves (a still-failing
reader rebuilds; a third reader's 4th failure after that rebuild does not), and the twin's
runtime held-SCL test now holds SCL at two phases against the readers' cycles.
