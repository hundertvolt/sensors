U35: the FRAM hold fixed at its source; the UART resyncs without waiting on FRAM

FRAM chunk layer (asy_fram_manager.py), by removal: a write computes its CRC once, before
either block's session, instead of once per copy inside it; a block read releases the
session before its CRC check; a cross-check reads the other block whole and compares it
with ==, its CRC only on a mismatch. check_length, the 8-byte slice loop, its per-slice
sleep and the chunk state they kept go. A block operation now holds the FRAM session and
bus locks across its two command yields alone, whatever the chunk size (a 264-byte chunk
held them 266-307 scheduler rounds per block before). The stored format is unchanged.

UART logging (asy_print_log.py, asy_uart_comm.py, asy_uart_driver.py): err_q()/wrn_q()
record and print an entry at once and leave its FRAM write to one writer task per store;
the UART's faults and its declined-command and drain-bound warnings use them, so a resync
starts at once whatever another FRAM user holds. A full heap at the writer's start prints
its text and leaves the entry for the next write; _err() stays a coroutine. The routine
resync line prints the bytes it drained.

Docs: F.3 hold.fram_block with its real waiters, the premise of its 2026-09-26 keep that
did not hold and the rule now in force; the same for the CRC yield's 2026-09-18 ruling
(I.2); C.7.1, G.2 and J.5 for the two logging forms; C.8, F.5.8 and I.2 for the hold and
the buffers; changelog B97.
