## The cause, in one line
Every FRAM block operation holds the FRAM driver's session lock plus the SPI bus lock (`asy_fram_driver.py:140-150`) across one scheduler round per CRC byte (`asy_crc_checks.py:47,71`) and per 8-byte compare slice (`asy_fram_manager.py:300`), so a hold costs (bytes x round time), not physics. Rounds held per operation (C, counted from the code): log-entry write 14 per block; SGP40 backup write 266 per block, 532 per backup; a read of the 268-byte block 270; each compare/verify of it 307 (34 slices x (8 CRC + 1 sleep) + 1); the hourly verify backup 1,146. The physics for one 528-byte backup: two 268-byte transfers at 8 MHz (~0.3 ms each) plus ~5 CS envelopes per block at <=3.4 ms (`stall.fram_command`) plus one CRC32 of CPU (below): ~50-70 ms (I). Measured: 8.3-19.7 s loaded (100-400x over), 0.5-1.06 s idle (7-20x over) (C, 12.89).
