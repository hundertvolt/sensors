## The UART hang found and fixed (agent, 2026-10-29, 14:59-15:20 UTC; commit 916fb878)

- **Cause:** `asy_uart_driver.py` stopped its receive ring with `DMA.active(False)` on the data channel, which is
  chained to the reload channel. rp2's `active(False)` is the SDK's `dma_channel_abort()`: it writes CHAN_ABORT, then
  spins in C until the channel is no longer busy. The abort completes the channel, the completion fires the chain, the
  reload restarts it, and the spin never ends; USB stops and the watchdog resets the board. Located with step markers in
  the watchdog's SCRATCH0 register (they survive the reset): the last step reached was `_rx_dma.active(False)` in
  `deinit()`. A probe confirmed it: aborting the data channel alone, or both channels together, leaves it busy;
  pointing its chain at itself first (`DMA.ctrl`, the non-triggering AL1_CTRL alias) stops both at once.
- **Reach in the product:** every `deinit()` or re-`init()` of a driver whose ring is armed (a task restart, a reconfig)
  hangs the firmware until the watchdog, reading as an unexplained watchdog reset. `rp2.DMA` is documented and not marked
  experimental (since v1.21), but its docs say nothing about aborting a chained channel.
- **Fix (bench branch; to carry to the audit branch):** `UART._rx_stop()` unchains, then aborts reload and data, used by
  `_arm_rx()` and `deinit()`. `tests/rp2.py`'s DMA fake now raises where silicon hangs (16 driver tests fail on the old
  driver); driver tests 174/174, comm tests 205/205, ruff clean; `UART_C_PORT_CHANGELOG.md` B93. mypy, the remaining
  tiers and the twin were not run.
- **Reruns with the fix, real watchdog:** all eight mounted UART scripts reached DONE.
  - idle poll rate: fast 1,233 / 1,227 rounds, idle 59 / 59 in 3 s (60 expected); `poll_idle_ms` works as designed.
  - recovery: the silence, desync and size-mismatch cases fail and recover, in 1.51 s (none) and 1.71-1.81 s (crc16)
    against the 7 s bound; log entry E81; no task exceptions.
  - exchange (none) and hazards (crc16) reached DONE; hazards' `stalled_tx`: failed, recovered after 2 attempts (E82).
    crc16 again shows `next_ok` false after a cancelled read mid-train and `clean_after` false after the cmd sweep.
  - break frame (none): it fails, the next frame succeeds.
- **DMA ring under interrupts off, its test fixed too:** the script started its reader only after the whole stream
  (window plus 50 ms either side, ~1.2 kB) had gone out, so the 512 B ring overran at every window and the host test's
  "all intact within the bound" could never pass. The reader now drains while the stream runs. CRC none: a 3 ms window
  keeps all 23 frames intact with no overrun; 45, 100 and 400 ms windows (beyond the 44.4 ms bound) overrun, are
  reported, and the 4 frames after each window all arrive. With the ring's DMA request off, the window sets UARTRSR.OE
  and the first frame fails, the next succeeds. CRC16: every window, 3 ms included, loses frames (2 intact) with an
  overrun: the CRC16 reader is slower than line rate on a back-to-back stream (cf. the CRC row, ~460 us/byte), so the
  ring fills regardless of the window. The protocol never streams frames back to back (each waits for its ACK), so this
  limits the test's stream, not the link.
