## 0. The three things this pass adds, in short

1. **The I2C "no bus" state cannot happen on rp2.** The first scan traced the 26 `raise OSError("I2C bus not
   initialized")` sites to one root: a failed controller re-construction in `recover()` (REPORT §4.2: "Only a failed
   controller re-construction produces that state"). The pinned source shows that re-construction cannot fail on
   this port with the stored arguments. The whole bool contract, the 26 raises and the SPI copy have no target
   trigger: about 120 lines (S2-G1-1..3).
2. **Two "one mechanism per handler" families collapse into the shared base.** `report_if_fatal()` is owner-decided
   (review `c-stack-reboot`, status ok) but sits at 85 G1 handlers, 82 of which already hand the exception to a log
   call; one scan in the logger covers them (S2-G1-5, form only). The per-episode log flags overlay the owner's
   central newest-entry rule, and the config-read ones have exactly one reachable trigger (S2-G1-8).
3. **Refuted or unreachable triggers carry more code than the first scan counted as doubtful.** The refused-timer-arm
   family stands on V6's refutation, yet four of its rows were classed R (S2-G1-7); the pre-setup-entry merge is
   unreachable through the generated boot order (S2-G1-15); `setup()` was widened to bool in 19 G1 methods whose only
   product reader sits in the lost-FRAM chain (S2-G1-10).
