Each lane folder holds the lane's final report (`report.md`) with its working logs and probes; D and E also keep their
pause states. `lead/` holds the lead's builds, checks and records. Paths inside the logs name the scratch worktrees and
toolchain copies they ran in, and files over 100 KB are gzipped. Product source was left out. Of the files picked, 12
were refused by the checks against the tracked tree: 4 by blob, 8 by content (90% or more of their lines in one tracked
file) and none by name. Each was a scratch copy of a tracked file. Six more passed the checks but were left out: a WIP
piece of the overrides module (89% in the tracked file), lane D's SPEC draft (85%), a stand-in for lane T's then-unmerged
API, two probes whose output was not kept, and a name list. Three near-duplicate reruns were replaced at assembly by a
diff against the kept final log, and each diff rebuilds its log exactly. The tick image's 10 MB disassembly was replaced
by an extract of every line with the offset literal. The bulk left out is the tick image and other binaries, the rp2
symbol listing, two preprocessor outputs, duplicates, empty markers and caches. `EXCLUDED.txt` lists each refused file
with its match, then the bulk, then the credential grep, which found no credential.
