U34 lane N: UDPSocket loses write_and_recvfrom(), which nothing calls

src/asy_udp_socket.py: write_and_recvfrom() and its range(tries) guard
leave. No src/ caller remains: the resolver checks its own write() before
each recvfrom() (asy_dns_client.py, whose tries guard already catches
OverflowError as well as TypeError), the NTP client does the same once
(asy_ntp_client.py), and the captive DNS uses recvfrom()/sendto(). The
frozen asy_udp_socket.mpy shrinks from 2195 to 2056 bytes (mpy-cross
v1.29.0, -march=armv6m); nothing else in the image changes.

tests/test_asy_udp_socket.py, in the same commit:
- the method's own tests leave (the retry-budget section, tries=0 and
  the malformed-tries sentinel), and with them the two tunables only
  they read, l1.asy_udp_socket_attempt_timeout_ms and
  l1.asy_udp_socket_exhaust_timeout_ms;
- the three NTP-pattern tests now drive the call shape
  asy_ntp_client.py's _fetch_ntp_reply() uses: write() checked, then
  recvfrom() into the shipped 48-byte buffer (_NTP_PACKET_LEN, read from
  source), disconnect() in finally, asserting (sent, reply, torn down);
  the garbage reply is one 27-byte copy, inside that buffer;
- the section, zero-length and malformed-input comments name the
  callers' own checks instead of the removed method.

Effect per mode: STA (NTP, DNS) and AP (captive DNS) keep their own
unchanged write()/recvfrom()/sendto() calls; Silent and Off send no UDP;
USB is unaffected. The method had no caller, so no mode loses a path.

SPECIFICATION.md still carries the two tunables' register rows and the
method's mention; they leave with that file's own change.
