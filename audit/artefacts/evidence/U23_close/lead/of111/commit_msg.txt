U23 lead: ResetErrors is refused once a reboot or erase command is accepted (OF-111)

WebserverService records, one-way, that a reboot, bootloader, resetconfig or erasefram command answered
"Valid" (_run_system_cmd(): set before the task yields, so no request runs between SystemService's
acceptance and the record); from then _put_status() answers ResetErrors "Failed" and starts no reset, so no
FRAM-backed log, RAM ring or HTTPDropped window is cleared and the logs survive into the reboot. Before, a
ResetErrors between the acceptance and S3's quiesce answered "Valid" and cleared every log on the chip, and
one after it answered "Failed" yet cleared every RAM ring. The record is the webserver's own because the
acceptance state (SystemService._shutdown) is not reachable from the routes without new wiring. mempause, a
refused or raising command and a word that is no command leave ResetErrors open.

Tests: one unit test over the four words (seen failing on the old code for all four: "Valid", two resets,
every log and the drop count cleared), a guard for mempause and the commands that were not accepted, and the
shared shutdown scenario back to "Failed" at every step S1-S6 for every word on all six devices, every error
log and both blocks of every log chunk on the chip compared around the PUT (seen failing on every device at
its first word, reboot: "Valid" at S1-S3, logs cleared at S1 and S4).

Owner decision (owner, 2026-10-13: "1 b"), on the question asked in session: "OF-111: refuse
`ResetErrors` once a reboot or erase command has been accepted? The case: between a reboot or erase
command's acceptance and the moment FRAM writes pause, a `ResetErrors` still answers "Valid" and clears
every FRAM-backed error log right before the reboot - the one evidence a reboot keeps. Every device with
FRAM. (a) As is: accepted until FRAM pauses. (b) Refuse it ("Failed") from the command's acceptance on:
one line in the status PUT handler; the logs survive into the reboot."
