U23 lead: a section switch lets the left section's GET on the wire finish instead of resetting it

Leaving a section aborted its in-flight GET, so the browser reset a connection the device was still
answering and the device logged a peer reset (W48) into its web-server error count on ordinary
navigation (agent, 2026-10-13, decided on the owner's behalf for review). The section's stop now
drops its queued GETs at once; its GET on the wire runs to its end, discarded (nothing painted, no
banner, no log, the device clock untouched), when another section is rendered into the same element
in the same turn. With no section following - the page's own stop before a reload - it is still
aborted, together with any GET it took over from an earlier switch. pollManager.request() gains a
fifth argument, a queue signal that drops a request only while it waits its turn.

The render test that pinned the next section's first GET not queuing behind the left one pinned the
abort itself; it is replaced by the switch case (seen failing first: the left fetch's signal was
aborted); a new case stops the page right after a switch and needs the taken-over GET aborted (seen
failing first). The page-stop case now also asserts the abort (a guard, bitten by a planted no-abort
defect). Live, wozi, eight switches each with a /status GET on the wire after ResetErrors: before,
8 of 8 aborted (net::ERR_ABORTED) and the page showed "Web Server: 8"; after, every GET ran to its
end and every row read 0. The next section's first GET now waits for the left one: median 216.6 ms
after the click against 6.6 ms before (one /status on the twin, at most the 15 s request cap).
