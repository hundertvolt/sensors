U23 lead: the live full-ceiling tabs check prints its counts on every run

run kept no evidence of how many tabs needed their reload. It now prints one line per device before
it asserts, on a pass and a failure alike: the tab count, the tabs loaded on the first pass, the
tabs loaded on their reload and the tabs not loaded at all. First run (wozi and the five others):
6 tabs, 5 on the first pass, 1 on its reload, 0 not at all, on every device.
