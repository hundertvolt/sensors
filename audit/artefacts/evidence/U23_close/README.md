# U23 close evidence

Each lane folder holds the lane's hand-back text (`handbacks.txt`, its WIP and final reports from
`u23/close/lane*_reports.txt`) with its working logs, probes and tools; each lead helper's folder holds its hand-back as
`handback.txt` (`u23/close/laneW1h_reports.txt`, `laneAPI2h_reports.txt`, `laneAPI5h_reports.txt`; for API4, round 2,
OF-111, OF-129 and its follow-up, round 4 and round 5 the same `extract_handbacks.py` over the helper's transcript). A
report's scratch path maps to this tree: `u23/laneD/` to `lane_d/` (and `u23d_tools/pdftext.py` to `lane_d/tools/`);
`u23/m_runs/` to `lane_m/runs/`; `u23/laneB/` to `lane_b/`; `u23/laneT/` to `lane_t/`; `u23c/` to `lane_c/`;
`u23/laneS/`, `laneX/`, `laneY/` to `lane_s/`, `lane_x/`, `lane_y/`; `u23/api1/`, `w1/`, `api2/`, `api3/`, `api4/`,
`api5/`, `leadedits/`, `of111/`, `of129/`, `round4/`, `round5/` to `lead/` under the same names, `u23/lead2/` to
`lead/round2/` and `u23/of129p/` to `lead/of129/of129p/`; `u23/close/f12/` to `lead/f12/`. Paths inside the logs name the
scratch worktrees and exports they ran in, and files over 100 KB are gzipped. Product source was left out. Two working
copies are kept as their diff against the file they were cut from: the lwIP bite (`lead/api1/bite/mutation.diff.txt`,
against `4f60381`, with its `bite.log`) and `js/render.js` as it stood at round 2's live probe
(12:09, `lead/round2/e1_render.diff.txt`, against `176a88c`); round 4's three plants are its own diffs (`plant_*.diff`). Files
whose lines all repeat tracked lines are kept as descriptions instead (lane D's seen-failing copy,
`lead/f12/swapped_file.txt`, `lead/api3/dropped_rows.txt`, `lead/api4/dropped_rows.txt`,
`lead/round2/traced_live_matrix_command.txt`) or left out (lane C's `edits/e2_new.py`, round 2's `render_tests_new.js`).
`EXCLUDED.txt` lists each file the checks refused, then the copies left out by construction, then the credential review.
`lead/RUNS.md.gz`, `lead/lead_todo.md` and `lead/CONCURRENCY.md` are snapshots from the staging (2026-10-13 14:15);
`assemble.sh` refreshes them and the final-now folders, fills each slot below only while it holds no file (a filled
slot is kept as it is) and leaves `gates/` to the gate.
