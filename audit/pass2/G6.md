### G6/R13 Recovery by quiesce, resync and deadlines
- **Req**: On any fault, local mid-train aborts included, the noticing side drains through the one `_fault()` helper until the line is quiet for `1.5 × timeout`, bounded at a multiple of that window, then holds off initiating for another `1.5 × timeout` (both Class A); a half-received frame is abandoned after `timeout`.
