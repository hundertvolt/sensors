### G1/R14 Reset oracles read reset_reason, not reset_cause
- **Req**: On RP2040 `machine.reset_cause()` reads `WDT_RESET` after a watchdog starvation, after `machine.reset()` and after `mpremote reset` (itself `machine.reset()` through a raw-REPL exec) alike, and `PWRON_RESET` otherwise, so no hardware oracle uses it to tell an intended reset from a starvation.
