# A-C3 Part R — requirements trace (end-state check, 2026-10-01)

Audit working file (deleted with `audit/` at phase D). Read-only on the M files and the register: every finding names the
M-ID (or the register block) to amend and the exact amendment text; the lead applies them after A-C2.

## Method

- **Inputs.** The 547 register blocks (`audit/pass2/G1-G10.md`, `LEAD.md`, `REF.md`); every unit file's ledger
  (`audit/actions/*.md`, "register block | clause | result", 1,181 rows) and every action's Why slot; the 1,865 merged
  changes of the 16 `M_*.md` (From slots, plus each M file's ledger table); AC_NOTES 1-50; the unapplied register fixes of
  U35, U36a, U36b, U37 and C (`REGISTER_FIXES_wave3.md`: "U35, U36a, U36b, U37 and C are not processed").
- **Tool.** `audit/sweeps/ac3_req_trace.py` (helper, re-runnable): requirement → ledger rows and Why-citing actions →
  merged changes whose From carries each action (a From mention marked dropped/withdrawn does not count; an M ledger row
  "merged into M.x" does) → the Unit slots of those changes. `python3 audit/sweeps/ac3_req_trace.py <out.json> --appendix
  <out.md> --findings <map.json>` regenerates the full trace (JSON) and the appendix table below.
- **Checks, per requirement.** (1) every unit its State names has a delivering action (ledger row, Why citation or an
  action of that unit named in the State); (2) every such action is carried by a merged change, and at least one carrier
  lands in the action's own unit (stages count); (3) process actions (B3 campaign, U32/U36/U37 procedures) are carried as
  steps, not only as file fragments or Depends; (4) the Home names a place that exists at the end state and that the
  delivering changes write; (5) a "holds" requirement is not contradicted by a merged change at its cited site;
  (6) ledger rows left "pending", "NOT-DONE" or "parked for A-C" were settled by a merge; (7) the six merged blocks
  (LEAD.md §2) are carried by their targets.
- **Scope note (OR107.a).** Every one of the 547 blocks went through checks (1)-(2) and (7) mechanically and every
  mechanical flag was read by hand (≈ 140 flags; most were false positives of the parser — e.g. a carrier citing an item
  by description, an action of another unit named in the State as context). Checks (3)-(6) were run for every flagged
  block and every "holds" block; check (4) beyond the flagged Homes was run file-by-file only where a Home names a moved
  section or a file no change writes. Item-level coverage (each numbered item of an action carried) was checked for all
  24 actions whose carriers cite items by number only; 22 were fully carried, the other two are findings R-01 and R-04.
  Not re-done: a sentence-by-sentence comparison of each delivering change's text with its requirement's Req (the merges'
  own adherence reads did that per file); the Site trace is Part S's.
