### G8.002 A near-miss tag fails the build
- **Req**: A comment tag that is present, or close to present with a typo, a dropped element or a wrong location, is verified in every dimension or fails the build loud; it is never read as "no tag here". Every tag family is built on the one shared scanner (`buildgen/tag_comments.py`) with its own shape predicate, and each family has the full accept/reject grammar test matrix SPEC L.5 lists. Accepted cost: prose shaped like a tag with its `@` sigil fails the build; a sigil-less typo and a bare tag word used as prose stay silent.
- **Class**: tooling
- **Sources**: GEN.N059, GEN.N060, GEN.N064, GEN.N075, GEN.N155, GEN.N156, GEN.N157, GEN.N158, GEN.N180, GEN.N181, GEN.N205, GEN.N208, GEN.N226, GEN.N251, GEN.N048, GEN.N152 · SPEC L.5 (6369-6416), H.5.1 (4426-4428); `buildgen/tag_comments.py:1-3,21,238-262`
- **Provenance**: O-confirmed — `12d62b6` (2026-09-10): "New standing rule (project owner's explicit direction)"
- **Truth**: partly — all five families import `check_for_near_miss_tags` from `tag_comments.py` (web_tag/limits/wiring/value_wiring/requires_tag :10-13); registration of a new family stays by convention (`tag_comments.py:21`); the column-0-inside-a-function heuristic (GEN.N063) can misread location
- **Fit**: extends OR16 (silent chains) and OR23 · pillar P5
- **Home**: SPEC L.5 → the OR44.a checklist cites it; stays in L.5
