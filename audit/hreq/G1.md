### G1.008 FRAM writes are not wear
- **Req**: FRAM writes are outside every wear gate and budget: both FRAM parts in use have 10^12 (MB85RS64V, wozi) or 10^13 (MB85RS2MTA, dev) read/write cycles per byte, effectively unbounded at this project's rates. This exempts FRAM from wear accounting only, not from the evidence rules (G1.024, G1.025).
- **Class**: hardware-safety
- **Sources**: HW.N008, HW.N270, HW.N404, HW.N749 · stated at CLAUDE.md wear rule ("FRAM is out of scope"); conftest.py:69-88; device_scripts/fram_same_device_rw_concurrency.py:2-3; flash/test_bus_concurrency.py:111-114
- **Provenance**: O — OR49.a "FRAM writes do not count as wear" (2026-09-26); CLAUDE.md wear rule
- **Truth**: holds — MB85RS2MTA "High endurance: 10^13 times/byte" (MB85RS2MTA datasheet p.1 and p.18 table); MB85RS64V 10^12 (p.1, dstxt:25); dev.toml:117-119 is MB85RS2MTA
- **Fit**: restates OR49.a (2) · pillar P9
- **Home**: CLAUDE.md → unchanged; datasheet figures to SPECIFICATION.md Part F (OR29.a (3))

## Questions for the owner

1. **Should the firmware mark its own intended resets?**
